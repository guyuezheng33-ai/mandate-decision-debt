## What the app does

Mandate reads the Jira evidence a signed-in user can already see and computes a deterministic
authority receipt. It is an evidence aid, not legal, compliance or investment advice. A score or
verdict is an indicator derived from the evidence that was visible, not a guarantee that a decision
was valid.
